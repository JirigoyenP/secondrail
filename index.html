Your processor owns your customers.

Cards live in Stripe's, Shopify's or Whop's vault. When they drop you, every saved card goes with them. You re-ask your whole base and lose the renewals that never answer.

How we solve it

Cards are tokenized once into a vault you own. Every processor receives a token, never the card. Switch providers and nothing is lost. Leave Second Rail and the vault leaves with you.

One account, one point of failure.

All your volume runs through a single merchant account. A risk review, a chargeback spike or a policy change at one processor stops your entire revenue overnight.

How we solve it

You connect several processors. We distribute volume across them by rules you set and keep every account healthy. When one terminates you, traffic moves to the others in minutes, not weeks.

Renewals fail and nobody knows why.

Many subscription declines are recoverable, but your platform shows a red X and moves on. Expired cards, soft declines and issuer blocks all look the same.

How we solve it

Every response code is captured and shown. Retries follow the decline reason, not a fixed schedule. Network tokens and account updater refresh expired cards before the next bill. You watch the decline rate fall, by reason, by processor.
